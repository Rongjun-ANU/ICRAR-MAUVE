## NGC4501

NGC4501 shows a clear positive correlation between $Z_\mathrm{gas}$ and $\Sigma_\mathrm{SFR}$. 

![image-20251013190402992](assets/image-20251013190402992.png)

Here I try taking the median value as the standard (offset 0) and then perform the offset map to see how the $\Sigma_\mathrm{SFR}$ and $Z_\mathrm{gas}$ vary across the galaxy.  For NGC4501, we can see in general, redder on the LHS also shows redder on the RHS. However, we also notice the spatial variation: the inverse correlation in this relatively low-mass HII regions.

## NGC4192

NGC4192 shows an ambigus correlation between $Z_\mathrm{gas}$ and $\Sigma_\mathrm{SFR}$?
