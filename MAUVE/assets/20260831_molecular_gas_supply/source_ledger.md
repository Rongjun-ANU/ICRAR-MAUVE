## Evidence conventions

- **Primary full text / relevant sections:** consequential equations, methods or instrument tables were checked in the original paper, author version, institutional copy or official documentation.
- **Primary abstract / metadata:** supports only the narrow abstract-level finding or bibliographic identity, not unaccessed detailed modelling.
- **Derived here:** algebra, units, budget definitions, identifiability arguments, experimental design and synthetic examples are this report's derivations or proposals, not published MAUVE measurements.
- The five-facility inventory and MUSE 4800-9300 Angstrom window come from the user's direct message. No actual product metadata were supplied. Programme benchmarks must not be substituted for delivered beams, sensitivities or filters.

## Derived statements and verification

The exact reservoir dictionary, factor-of-two convention, neutral-phase cancellation, time-derivative product rule, metallicity algebra, coherent-flow sign, beam nonlinearity, error propagation and worked examples are implemented in `verify_equations.py`. `verification_math.json` records 27 passing checks. Correct algebra does not validate a density closure, stellar-return fraction, phase mixing, stationarity or a CO conversion factor for a particular MAUVE region.

The recommended claim hierarchy, two-resolution strategy and observation priorities are the report's synthesis. They explicitly follow the missing parameter for each chosen scientific target. They are not source quotations, observing proposals with exposure forecasts, or asserted properties of files that were not supplied.

## Search and access limitations

Discovery followed five lanes: resolved/cloud accretion; H I-H2 transition chemistry; chemical fluorescence diagnostics; cloud/stellar lifecycle clocks; and instrument resolution with H I alternatives. Follow-up verification targeted plausible confusions rather than manufactured search-result counts. Older full articles, author versions and official instrument pages were preferred to secondary summaries.

Some original ADS/DOI/publisher endpoints failed transiently. Accessible author/institutional versions were used, and narrow abstract-only use is labelled above and in Appendix C. Not every metadata record was available as readable full text. No PRISMA-style completeness claim, citation-ranking claim or blanket live-link validation is made.

One read-only worker, `/root/resolution_evidence`, checked instruments/surveys, selected publication metadata and editorial/provenance consistency. Native handles not retained in this ledger are not fabricated; the primary locators and access limits remain explicit. The primary agent retained interpretation, scientific definitions, unit choices, uncertainty propagation, equation checks and final acceptance.
