# 20260821 rSFMS and rMZR with Infall Stages within Effective Radius

First i also want to look at the HII spaxel count's normalized distribution along $\Sigma_*$. Again, we can confirm that for starforming regions, in pre-peak stages tend to be in low-$\Sigma_*$ range, in post-peak tend to be in high-$\Sigma_*$ range, while close-to-peak is hard to tell.

![image-20260820151621703](assets/image-20260820151621703.png)

Then we can look at the HII spaxel within $R_e$. Here i adopt NGVS g-band curve-of-growth $R_e$ when available, and then GOLDMine H-band $R_e$. For NGC4064 I use VERTICO IV stellar half-mass radius $R_{e,*}=4.27\pm1.05$ kpc. Below i show the boundary of $R_e$, with galatic center adopted from SIMBAD, major and minor axis and PA from VERTICO I.

![image-20260820153028141](assets/image-20260820153028141.png)
